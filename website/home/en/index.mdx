## Additive by design

UltraCode does not replace OpenCode, change providers or models, or read credentials. It adds roles, workflows, fresh-context loops, and visual QA while preserving existing configuration.

```bash
curl -fsSL https://project-ultracode.williamhvollita.workers.dev/install.sh | bash
```

- Provider and model neutral
- Idempotent maximum-union merging
- Official OpenCode upgrades remain available
- SHA-256 checksums and build provenance
