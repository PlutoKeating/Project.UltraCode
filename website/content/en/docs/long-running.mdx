## How Long-Running Work Feels in Practice

- **Steady, uncluttered progress**: UltraCode moves from one milestone to the next automatically while keeping your chat concise — only milestones, blockers, and verified results.
- **Instant steering at any time**: Sending any new message immediately redirects or pauses execution, so you always stay in charge.
- **Seamless next-day resumption**: Close your terminal or desktop window, restart, or come back tomorrow — just open `ultracode` and say:

```text
Read the handoff and continue
```

UltraCode picks up your exact progress, what was already verified, and what comes next.
