## Talk to UltraCode Like a PM or Architect

No magic prompt tricks or slash commands required. Just explain three things in plain language:

- **Who it is for**: the people using it and where they use it (desktop, mobile, terminal, etc.).
- **What it should do**: the core flow, look-and-feel preferences, and anything explicitly out of scope.
- **How to know it is done**: your real acceptance bar (passing tests, clean mobile layout, reliable edge cases).
