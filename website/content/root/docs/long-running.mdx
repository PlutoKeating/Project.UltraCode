## How Long-Running Work Works in Practice

- **Steady, focused progress**: UltraCode advances from one milestone to the next automatically while keeping routine messages concise — reporting milestones, blockers, and verified outcomes.
- **Instant steering at any time**: Sending any new message immediately redirects or pauses execution, keeping you in control.
- **Clean resumption across sessions**: Close your terminal or desktop app and come back later — simply run `ultracode` and say:

```text
Read the handoff and continue
```

UltraCode restores the saved progress, verified evidence, and next planned step directly from your repository.
