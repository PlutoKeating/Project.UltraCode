This lesson bundles three small things that usually show up together: **advance on its own, resume tomorrow, and spend less.**

## <span class="uc-step-no">01</span> Give it a goal that can advance on its own

`/goal` suits work that **splits into several steps, each of which can be verified**. If one sentence cannot hold the request, put it in the goal.

<CommandBlock
  label="Start a multi-step goal with this"
  command="/goal Turn this sign-up page into something shippable: add an empty-slot hint, add the confirmation card after submitting, re-check the phone layout, and hand it over once everything passes."
  mono={false}
  copyLabel="Copy"
  copiedLabel="Copied"
/>

**What you'll see:** It records the goal, then moves from one small completed step to the next until every check passes.

## <span class="uc-step-no">02</span> Pause or resume with one line

You can also just send a new message to interrupt — your words always outrank automatic progress.

<CommandBlock
  label="When you want it to stop"
  command="/goal pause"
  copyLabel="Copy"
  copiedLabel="Copied"
/>

**What you'll see:** It stops on the current step instead of editing further; swap pause for resume to continue.

## <span class="uc-step-no">03</span> Close the laptop, continue tomorrow

Progress is not trapped in a chat log: it is written into handoff files in your project, so a new window or a new day can pick it up.

<CommandBlock
  label="The next day, this is all you need"
  command="Read the handoff notes and continue from yesterday's progress."
  mono={false}
  copyLabel="Copy"
  copiedLabel="Copied"
/>

**What you'll see:** It first says what was finished, what was verified, what is left, then continues from the next step instead of restarting.

## <span class="uc-step-no">04</span> With several models, let each role do what it is good at

If you only have one model configured, skip this step — every role simply uses your current model.

**What you'll see:** A short recommendation appears, and one confirmation writes it in. Planning and review get the stronger model; quick lookups get the faster, cheaper one.

That covers the whole loop: clarify, build, verify, and carry it across days. Start again from [the desktop walkthrough](/docs/tutorial-desktop/) whenever you want a refresher.
