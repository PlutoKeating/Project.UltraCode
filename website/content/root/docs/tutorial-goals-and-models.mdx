This lesson bundles three small things that usually show up together: **advance on its own, resume tomorrow, and spend less.**

<TutorialStep
  step={1}
  title="Give it a goal that can advance on its own"
  promptLabel="Start a multi-step goal with this"
  promptText="/goal Turn this sign-up page into something shippable: add an empty-slot hint, add the confirmation card after submitting, re-check the phone layout, and hand it over once everything passes."
  copyLabel="Copy"
  copiedLabel="Copied"
  expectLabel="What you'll see"
  expectText="It records the goal, then moves from one small completed step to the next until every check passes."
>
  `/goal` suits work that **splits into several steps, each of which can be verified**. If one sentence cannot hold the request, put it in the goal.
</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep
  step={2}
  title="Pause or resume with one line"
  promptLabel="When you want it to stop"
  promptText="/goal pause"
  isCommand={true}
  copyLabel="Copy"
  copiedLabel="Copied"
  expectLabel="What you'll see"
  expectText="It stops on the current step instead of editing further; swap pause for resume to continue."
>
  You can also just send a new message to interrupt — your words always outrank automatic progress.
</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep
  step={3}
  title="Close the laptop, continue tomorrow"
  promptLabel="The next day, this is all you need"
  promptText="Read the handoff notes and continue from yesterday's progress."
  copyLabel="Copy"
  copiedLabel="Copied"
  expectLabel="What you'll see"
  expectText="It first says what was finished, what was verified, what is left, then continues from the next step instead of restarting."
>
  Progress is not trapped in a chat log: it is written into handoff files in your project, so a new window or a new day can pick it up.
</TutorialStep>

<TutorialStep
  step={4}
  title="With several models, let each role do what it is good at"
  promptLabel="Pair models for planning, coding, research, and visual checks"
  promptText="/setup-models"
  isCommand={true}
  copyLabel="Copy"
  copiedLabel="Copied"
  expectLabel="What you'll see"
  expectText="A short recommendation appears, and one confirmation writes it in. Planning and review get the stronger model; quick lookups get the faster, cheaper one."
>
  If you only have one model configured, skip this step — every role simply uses your current model.
</TutorialStep>

That covers the whole loop: clarify, build, verify, and carry it across days. Start again from [the desktop walkthrough](/docs/tutorial-desktop/) whenever you want a refresher.
